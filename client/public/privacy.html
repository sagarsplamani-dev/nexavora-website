Privacy policy

This page explains how information submitted through course enquiry, demo booking, and contact forms is used.

Information you submit

Forms may ask for your name, email address, phone number, course preference, and message. Submissions are stored to respond to an enquiry and coordinate a requested callback or demo.

Email and service providers

If email delivery is configured, the site may send a submission notification to the course administrator and a confirmation to the email address you provided. Email delivery is optional; a delivery failure does not remove a saved request.

Your choices

Do not submit information you do not want used to respond to your request. To ask about a submission or request its correction or deletion, use the website contact form and include enough detail to locate it.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. Nexavora should review the final legal text before public launch.
